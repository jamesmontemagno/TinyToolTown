For terminal-suckers, manage PRs, Pipelines, Workitems right in the terminal via a TUI.

Works with both Azure DevOps and Github. Multiprojects side by side, your work-projects side by side with your personal projects.

Hankering around in the browser and the somewhat clunky navigation in DevOps and GitHub now is available with just a key-press, filtering and toggling. Delightful if you ask me :)
