Barely is a daily tracker for people who are tired of to-do apps that treat you like an underperforming machine. Each morning you jot down at most three tiny things, split between work and life and that's the whole game. Making the bed counts, a two-minute email counts, and a rest day is a perfectly valid entry. 

I built it because every other productivity app optimizes for more, and I wanted one that optimizes for **Enough** that is something kind to you on the days when "crushing it" simply isn't on the menu. 

It's delightful because it celebrates showing up instead of guilt-tripping you about streaks, it works offline and keeps your data entirely on your device (no account, no nagging), and it quietly proves the little truth everyone knows deep inside: 1% is still progress, and barely is still something.
