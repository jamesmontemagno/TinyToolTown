DragToDesktop is a lightweight Windows 11 tray app that brings a Snap-Layouts-style flyout to virtual desktop management. Just drag a window to the right edge of your screen, hold for a moment, and drop it onto a new or existing desktop. It's multi-monitor aware, requires no admin rights or installer, and runs self-contained right from your system tray.
