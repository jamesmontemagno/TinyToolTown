I simply got tired of putting all my temp notes into a NP++ or new Sublime tab. So I built QuinSlate - quick, jot-focused notepad for Windows.

It sits in the tray, can be manipulated using keyboard only and has an inline calculator to do quick math while on a call. It’s just 5 fixed scratchpads and that's it. Intentionally dead simple and focused on one specific thing.
