This small, dependency-free browser tool estimates buyer price, seller proceeds, and separate fee components in integer minor units. It was built to make rounding and minimum-fee boundaries visible instead of hiding them behind a single total. It runs entirely in the browser, requires no account, and includes a disclaimer to confirm the final amount in Steam because rates and currency rules can change.
