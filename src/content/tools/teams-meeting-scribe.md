Teams Meeting Scribe sits quietly in your system tray waiting for Teams calls, records them, transcribes them and puts the transcription into your Obsidian vault (or elsewhere). 100% free and local.

Built this because all the alternatives were paid and/or bloated massive web apps in a desktop shell. This thing is 100% pure .NET and takes 14MB RAM idle.
