When you're working with a team that's widely spread geographically, sometimes it can be frustrating to know what time zone someone is it. The Windows calendar flyout only shows your own, plus two more. I often find myself needing more. Time Zone Zone lets you show as many as will fit on your monitor, and scroll as many as you want beyond that.

You can also label each time zone, to remind you of who exactly is there!
