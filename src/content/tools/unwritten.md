Unwritten learns from your git history to detect when files that usually change together are missing from your edits. It works as an MCP server for AI coding agents (Claude Code, Copilot) to self-check mid-session, and as a CLI tool for pre-commit hooks. Bringing 20 years of change coupling research to the agent era—free, local, and transparent about confidence scores.
